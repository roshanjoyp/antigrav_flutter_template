.03

Production essentials

None of this is decoration. Each piece exists because shipping to a store — and surviving what comes after — demands it.

Build flavors

Dev, staging, and prod entry points with separate application ids and launcher labels, on Android and iOS. You cannot test push, payments, or auth against production data — and you can't debug a release without an environment that mirrors it. All three installs sit side by side on one device.

Crash reporting & analytics

After launch, the debugger is gone. Crashlytics and Analytics are the only eyes you have on a production app — what crashed, for whom, after which action. Both sit behind service interfaces with stub implementations, so tests and local runs stay offline.

Errors as values

Every repository returns Result<T>. In production, an unhandled exception is a frozen screen and a one-star review. Failures as values force every layer to decide — retry, message, or fallback — instead of crashing three layers up.

Real sign-in, store-compliant

Email/password, Google, Apple, and anonymous auth, with errors mapped into typed failures. Apple review requires Sign in with Apple the moment you offer any third-party login — it's wired, not a TODO.

Retention & revenue mechanics

Onboarding with persisted state, push deep links, subscription entitlements. Every shipping app grows into these; bolting them on later means rewiring startup, routing, and storage. Here they're already behind interfaces the rest of the app respects.

Verified releases

CI with analyze, format, tests, staleness-checked codegen, and Android/iOS build jobs — plus a production-readiness checklist the doctor enforces: icon replaced, splash branded, signing configured, store metadata written. Launch failures are usually config, not code. Config here is checkable.
